## Frameworks — Google mandates Workspace access for agents through an MCP gateway
On 11/24, Google GA'd 8 MCP servers for Workspace (general availability) and added a 'Workspace AI Tools Addendum' to its API terms of service. From 2027-02-01, AI agents are required to route through Google MCP servers to access Gmail, Drive, Calendar, etc., and direct REST calls will be limited to 'Standard' quotas regardless of tier. Agent masquerading to evade the rule is explicitly prohibited, and retention of Google Technical Information such as memory and embeddings is capped at 30 days with training, fine-tuning, and distillation also banned. — TechStackUps https://techstackups.com/articles/google-workspace-ai-agents-mcp/ (cross-verified 3)
**Insight**: This is the largest example of the access layer crossing over from a protocol standard (MCP) to a terms-of-service gateway. The 30-day retention cap directly and structurally constrains long-term memory agent products, and together with the gating moves of Apple and Walmart, 'agent gatekeeping' is solidifying as a new axis of platform competition. Whether other platforms (Microsoft, Meta) follow suit is a key point to watch.

## Industry — Nvidia temporarily halts AI server shipments to Southeast Asia
According to a WSJ report (cross-verified 4), Nvidia instructed manufacturing partners such as Dell and Super Micro to temporarily halt server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia for an indefinite period. The purpose is to evaluate regional partners' compliance with US export requirements, targeting the roundabout use of Southeast Asian remote datacenters by Chinese companies. According to an estimate by the think tank Institute for Progress, such remote datacenters supply about 24% of China's AI computing power. — TechCrunch (citing WSJ) https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/26/nvidia-hits-pause-on-chip-shipments-to-southeast-asia/
**Insight**: Export controls have moved from 'where the chip is located' to a tracking stage of 'who uses the chip remotely'. If the halt drags on, it becomes the first real-world case affecting both Southeast Asian datacenter expansion plans and the US-China AI computing power gap.

## Models — GPT-6.2 'cost-cutting tuning' controversy
According to analysis by the operator of agentduel.com, OpenAI's Astra 6.2 and Sol 6.2 showed a significant decrease in output and inference tokens per task compared to previous versions (output token decrease p-values: Sol 0.0055 / Astra 0.022, inference token decrease p-values: Sol 0.00013 / Astra 0.0064). The author interpreted this as tuning aimed at cost savings. — micjm.com https://micjm.com/blog/openai-is-in-cost-control-mode/ (community, cross-verified 2)
**Insight**: This is a hypothesis based on community measurement data and is not an independently verified conclusion. However, if the pattern of 'similar benchmark scores, reduced token spending' is replicated, it becomes the first body of evidence blurring the line between model improvement and service margin management. Note the trend of leaderboard and measurement communities acting as model auditors.

## Tools — bespoke inference engine boom and a self-hosting renaissance
As of November 2026, inference engine forks specialized for particular hardware and models have proliferated (Strata, Basalt, etc.). A pitfall is also pointed out where the pursuit of speed alters the model's expert-selection method. The author's llama-cpp-rdna-boosts patchset reported improvements of roughly 35 to 55 tokens per second for token generation and roughly 700 to 1300 tokens per second for prefill in an AMD environment (varies by hardware, YMMV explicitly stated). — devnull.land https://devnull.land/amd-specific-llama-cpp (community, cross-verified 2)
Separately, a long-time self-hosting user confesses that AI coding agents have reignited their passion for experimentation — because with Git-managed infrastructure, AI can handle infrastructure tasks like any ordinary project, opening the door to high-barrier technologies like NixOS. — janlukas.blog https://janlukas.blog/posts/ai-self-hosting (community, cross-verified 2)
**Insight**: The tangible impact of agents is increasingly felt not in the cloud or frontier, but on 'my server, my hardware'. As self-reported speed figures from personal blogs that have not been verified are used as market signals, demand for replication reports and benchmark standardization will grow alongside.
